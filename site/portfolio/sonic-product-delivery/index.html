01 / CONTEXT

The brief

Sonic’s Systems Operations Center connected software development with the infrastructure behind customer services. The project portfolio included an ACH payments integration, an eero mesh Wi-Fi service, and the launch of an IP broadband product group. Each involved both product decisions and operational delivery.

02 / RESPONSIBILITY

What I owned

  • Coordinate six full-stack developers and six systems administrators.
  • Manage product work for the ACH payments integration.
  • Manage projects for the eero Wi-Fi service and IP broadband product launch.

03 / EXECUTION

How the work was delivered

The role brought requirements, service design, and delivery planning into the same conversation. I worked across product and service design, design thinking, and UX/UI to connect customer-facing behavior with the supporting systems.

Planning used Jira with Scrum and Kanban alongside Waterfall projects. That mix reflected different delivery needs within the program. The central responsibility was coordination: helping software and systems specialists work toward shared product and launch objectives.

04 / EVIDENCE

Delivery and evidence

  • Coordinated a twelve-person group spanning development and systems administration.
  • Owned product or project responsibilities across payments, Wi-Fi, and broadband initiatives.
  • Connected service design with the teams responsible for implementation and operations.

THE OPERATING LESSON

A service launch reaches beyond the application. Product ownership needs to connect user experience, payment behavior, infrastructure, and the teams that will operate the service.

Tools & disciplines
