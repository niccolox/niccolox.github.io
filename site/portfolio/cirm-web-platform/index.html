01 / CONTEXT

The brief

The California Institute for Regenerative Medicine needed ongoing stewardship of a large public web estate. The work combined Drupal and WordPress operations with a website refresh and mobile-first redesign. Content structure, vendor coordination, and code maintenance were all part of the role.

02 / RESPONSIBILITY

What I owned

  • Manage content operations across more than 6,000 pages and fifteen content types.
  • Lead the search for a website refresh agency and manage vendor coordination.
  • Technically manage the redesign while maintaining code, documentation, and operational support.

03 / EXECUTION

How the work was delivered

I worked on landing pages, rich-content page building, and the technical delivery of the mobile-first redesign. The hosting and delivery environment included Acquia, Pantheon, Cloudflare CDN, and self-hosting. Product specifications and runbooks supported both planned changes and day-to-day operations.

Drupal code maintenance included responding to the vulnerability commonly known as Drupalgeddon. The portfolio records successful defensive maintenance during that period.

04 / EVIDENCE

Delivery and evidence

  • Maintained a public content estate of more than 6,000 pages with fifteen content types.
  • Coordinated the technical and vendor work behind a mobile-first website redesign.
  • Combined publishing support with documented operations and security maintenance.

THE OPERATING LESSON

A public website is an ongoing service. Redesign work is strongest when content ownership, maintainable publishing patterns, vendor accountability, and security operations are addressed together.
