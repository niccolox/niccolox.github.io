01 / CONTEXT

The brief

Mantra Resorts’ accommodation ecommerce work spanned the Mantra, BreakFree, and Peppers domains. The platform combined a single codebase with three distinct brand destinations and approximately 10,000 unique destination and hotel-room content items.

02 / RESPONSIBILITY

What I owned

  • Manage the full lifecycle from requirements and specification through acceptance, launch, and documentation.
  • Coordinate ten developers across a nine-month program.
  • Connect the multisite platform and custom module work with the accommodation ecommerce requirements.

03 / EXECUTION

How the work was delivered

The program covered requirements, specifications, task definition, development, testing, and acceptance before launch. The implementation used custom .NET/DotNetNuke modules and a shared codebase supporting the three domains.

The source slide records a nine-month delivery period, ten developers, and an AUD 500,000 project scale. The delivery sequence connected the shared platform with the destination and room content, testing, acceptance, and launch documentation.

04 / EVIDENCE

Delivery and evidence

  • Delivered accommodation ecommerce work across three domains using one codebase.
  • Coordinated a substantial destination and hotel-room content estate with custom application development.
  • Owned the delivery sequence from requirements to launch and documentation.

THE OPERATING LESSON

A shared platform can support multiple brands when the common system and the brand-specific requirements are made explicit. Delivery leadership connects that architecture to content, acceptance, and launch.

Tools & disciplines

  • Full SDLC
