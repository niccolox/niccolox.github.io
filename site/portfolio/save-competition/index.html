01 / CONTEXT

The brief

Save Competition was a Sonic initiative involving FCC advocacy and direct email campaigns. The delivery challenge connected a public-facing campaign with internal executive, creative, engineering, and communications work, as well as an external regulatory interface.

02 / RESPONSIBILITY

What I owned

  • Manage Agile development directly across the CEO, creative, developer, and communications teams.
  • Coordinate the digital campaign and its technical delivery.
  • Curate more than 9,000 citizen comments associated with the FCC campaign.

03 / EXECUTION

How the work was delivered

I worked as the programming project manager within Sonic, linking executive intent with the creative assets and development tasks needed for the campaign. The project combined campaign publishing and communication with a technical integration using Docker, AWS, and the FCC API.

The portfolio records the curation of more than 9,000 citizen comments. Content handling was therefore a substantial part of the project alongside the campaign experience and its technical integration.

04 / EVIDENCE

Delivery and evidence

  • Coordinated a cross-functional public-facing campaign project.
  • Curated more than 9,000 citizen FCC comments.
  • Connected campaign delivery with a Docker/AWS implementation and FCC API integration.

THE OPERATING LESSON

Public-facing campaigns need the same delivery discipline as other digital products: explicit ownership, clear handoffs, and a reliable connection between communication and implementation.

Tools & disciplines

  • Cross-functional delivery
  • Campaign operations
  • Agile
  • Docker
  • AWS
