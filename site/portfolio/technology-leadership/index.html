01 / CONTEXT

The work in context

The opening slide brings together three perspectives: directing technology, building software, and helping customers succeed. The work that follows spans wine ecommerce, financial-services software, cloud infrastructure, telecommunications, government publishing, hospitality, and news media.

02 / RESPONSIBILITY

What I owned

  • Connect business requirements with practical engineering and delivery decisions.
  • Coordinate developers, operations teams, vendors, and business stakeholders.
  • Stay involved through implementation, launch, support, and adoption.

03 / EXECUTION

Connecting the work

The commercial work ranges from large historical order migrations to customer onboarding and public-facing publishing. Across these settings, delivery involves both the software and the people who operate it: clear specifications, coordinated releases, useful documentation, and ownership after launch.

Independent work adds a founder’s perspective. Citizen Success combines a political geography database, publishing, subscriptions, payments, and self-hosted operations. The introductory slide also lists cultural projects, sustainability studies, and open-source event organizing; these are background interests rather than separate project slides in this PDF.

04 / EVIDENCE

What this portfolio shows

  • A portfolio that shows both hands-on implementation and cross-functional leadership.
  • Dedicated examples of commerce modernization, technical customer success, operational delivery, and publishing.

THE OPERATING LESSON

Technology leadership is most useful when strategy, implementation, and operating responsibility stay connected. These case studies show how I work across those boundaries.
