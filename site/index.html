HOW I WORK

Close to the business.
Close to the build.

I work across leadership, engineering, and operations. The job is to understand what needs to change, make a practical plan, and stay with it through adoption.

  1. 01

    Find the operating constraint.

    Start with the customer journey and the people doing the work. Map the data, handoffs, costs, and decisions.

  2. 02

    Prove the difficult connection.

    Use a focused proof of concept to expose integration limits and vendor dependencies before committing to the full roadmap.

  3. 03

    Make the change sustainable.

    Connect delivery with ownership, training, support, and measures the business can use.
