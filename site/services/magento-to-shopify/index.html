WHY MIGRATE / CUSTOMER TRUST & BUSINESS RISK

A missed patch can become
a customer trust problem.

An ageing Magento and PHP stack can turn maintenance into a recurring business constraint. If a vulnerability is exploited, the impact can reach customer records, daily sales, and the team responsible for recovery.

Exposure depends on your versions, patch history, extensions, and configuration. On a self-managed store, your team and providers must keep those layers current.

CUSTOMER CONFIDENCE

The difficult customer conversation.

Exposure of names, addresses, or order history can lead to worried calls and customers hesitating to buy again. Reassurance takes work long after a technical fix.

TRADING CONTINUITY

Campaigns pause. Orders still need attention.

Containment can mean disabling checkout or taking parts of the store offline. Paid traffic, fulfillment commitments, and customer support need decisions while the incident is investigated.

BUDGET & FOCUS

Recovery takes over the roadmap.

Emergency specialists, data checks, and customer communications can consume the budget and time intended for merchandising, conversion improvements, and new sales channels.

PHP’s end-of-life branches stop receiving upstream security fixes. PHP support policy

Adobe’s September 8, 2026 Commerce and Magento Open Source update addressed flaws including security bypass and privilege escalation. Adobe reported no known exploitation of those issues when it published the bulletin. Adobe security bulletin

THE ADVANTAGE OF A HOSTED PLATFORM

Move core maintenance off your team’s backlog.

A Shopify-hosted storefront replaces the customer-facing Magento/PHP application with a platform operated by Shopify. Your team no longer runs that Magento application and its PHP runtime for the storefront.

Shopify plans include commerce hosting and TLS/SSL certificates. Its Level 1 PCI DSS compliance covers the Shopify store, checkout, and hosting. Hosting details · PCI scope

That shift can free capacity for product launches, merchandising, and customer experience. The business case compares those priorities with the full cost of plans, apps, transactions, and integrations.

THE RESPONSIBILITIES YOU KEEP

Keep control of access and connected systems.

Your team still owns staff access, app permissions, API credentials, customer-data handling, and the custom systems connected to the store. Any retained PHP services still need support and patching.

Shopify recommends individual staff accounts and permissions, plus passkeys or two-step authentication. Apps and custom code need review too. Shopify account security · Custom pixel security

The migration plan includes access review, integration ownership, and the retirement of the old store after reconciliation and retention requirements are agreed.

Start with your actual PHP version, Magento patch level, and extension inventory. Keep urgent remediation moving while the migration is planned.

BEFORE YOU COMMIT

Questions worth
settling early.

Migration choices affect your customers and the people running your store. Discovery makes those decisions explicit.

How much does a migration cost, and how long does it take?

Catalog complexity, order history, custom workflows, integrations, and storefront requirements determine the work. After assessment, you receive a phased proposal with scope, fees, dependencies, and a delivery schedule.

Do I need Shopify Plus?

The assessment compares your requirements with Shopify’s current plans, app costs, and customization options. That comparison informs the recommendation before the build is scoped.

Can customers keep their Magento passwords?

Encrypted passwords are not imported into Shopify. The migration plan includes customer communications and the account sign-in or activation approach selected for your store. See Shopify’s migration checklist.

How do you handle SEO and existing URLs?

Important pages are mapped to their new destinations, with supported redirects, metadata checks, and testing before and after launch. Search performance can change during a migration; ranking guarantees are not part of the offer. Shopify’s redirect rules inform the plan.

What happens to custom Magento features?

Each workflow is reviewed with its business owner. The blueprint records what will use Shopify capabilities, what needs an app or custom integration, and what your team chooses to retire. Subscriptions, store credit, and specialist selling models are assessed individually.

Can you work with our team or existing agency?

Yes. The engagement can cover migration leadership or a defined delivery scope, such as data and integrations. Responsibilities, decision owners, and handover requirements are agreed at the start.
